How To Style Winter Whites

Redefine your winter wardrobe with the timeless elegance of winter whites with this style guide.

In a season dominated by dark hues, redefine your winter wardrobe with the timeless elegance of winter whites. Whether top-to-toe white outfits, tonal mixing-and-matching, or a key white piece (or two), give your style a breath of fresh air with this list of winter white closet essentials.

Nail the Classics

Do pure winter chic with a classic cashmere white sweater. Made in the softest cashmere, it's a sweater that will last season after season. Effortlessly elevating any winter outfit, a white sweater is a must for any capsule collection.

Monochromatic Magic

Nothing feels more luxe than an all-white winter outfit. Pair classic corduroy pants with a modern wide-legged silhouette and finish the look with subtle accessories and soft neutral layers.

Keep Warm in White

Stay warm all winter long with a white puffer jacket, layered over knitwear. Throw on a white coat over a neutral outfit for a polished winter look while keeping comfort and warmth.

Textures and Layers

Winter fashion is all about layering, and white outfits provide the perfect base for playing with textures and fabrics. Pair a soft knit sweater with tailored trousers, layer with a structured wool coat, or style a chunky cardigan over a turtleneck to create a timeless cold-weather look.

Accessorize with Neutrals

Neutral accessories perfectly complement an all-white wardrobe. Choose leather boots, soft scarves, structured handbags, and minimal jewellery to complete your look while maintaining an elegant and modern appearance.
